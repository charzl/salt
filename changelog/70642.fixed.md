Fixed a minion that stopped signing in to the master after a caller gave up
waiting for the sign-in while the master was unreachable. ``AsyncAuth``
callers no longer wait on a shared future. One task does the sign-in and the
callers wait for it to report that it is over, so cancelling a caller cannot
cancel the sign-in for the others, leave a second sign-in task running, or
make ``authenticated`` raise, and an unexpected error in the sign-in reaches
every waiting caller. ``AsyncAuth.authenticate()`` is now a coroutine.
