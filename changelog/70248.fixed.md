Fixed master metrics recorded in MWorker processes (``salt.jobs.published``, ``salt.jobs.completed``, ``salt.master.requests.handled``, ``salt.master.requests.duration``, ``salt.auth.attempts``) being lost under the Prometheus exporter and overwritten under OTLP when the master runs more than one worker. Job counters are now derived from ``salt/job/`` events in the master parent. Request and auth metrics are aggregated in each worker and sent to the parent every ``metrics.worker_flush_interval_seconds`` (default 10) as ``salt/metrics/worker/<name>`` events.
