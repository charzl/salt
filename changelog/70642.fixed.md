Fixed a minion that stopped signing in to the master after a caller gave up
waiting for the sign-in while the master was unreachable. ``AsyncAuth``
callers no longer share one future: they take turns on a lock and each caller
runs the sign-in in its own task, so cancelling one caller cannot cancel the
sign-in for the others, leave a sign-in task running in the background, or make
``authenticated`` raise. ``AsyncAuth.authenticate()`` is now a coroutine.
