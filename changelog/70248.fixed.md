Fix metrics from forked master processes overwriting each other. Every process now reports a distinct ``service.instance.id`` and ``process.pid`` resource attribute (``master-main``, ``mworker-<pool>-<index>``), so OTLP series from different MWorkers no longer collide. With the ``prometheus`` exporter each process binds its own port (``metrics.prometheus.port`` plus its index, within ``metrics.prometheus.port_range``) instead of every MWorker failing to bind the same port and dropping its metrics.
